Two Lenses on Refusal

J-lens and R-lens are the same Jacobian estimator over the same forward pass — RelP only changes how gradients flow backward, through an LRP-modified graph. Same target layer, same corpus, same 25 prompts. So when the two columns below disagree, the difference is the lens, not the input. Both read one recovered refusal direction, at every layer. RelP claims better faithfulness on early layers; here is what that looks like.

The direction was not published — it was recovered. Abliteration removes a direction by projecting it out of the weights, so W_base − W_abliterated is rank one and its leading left singular vector is the direction. Measured s₁/‖D‖F was 0.997 at layer 11 and 0.996 at layer 31, and the two independently recovered vectors agree at |cos| = 1.0000 — one global direction, confirmed twice. SVD fixes no sign, and the semantics turned out to sit on −r; +r is flat noise at near-maximum entropy.
