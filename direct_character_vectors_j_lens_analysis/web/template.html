Qwen3-32B · Jacobian lens

The Bard Direction

Every layer of Qwen3-32B carries a direction that means bard. The Jacobian lens transports each one into the model's output basis and decodes it with the model's own unembedding — so you can read what the character is disposed to say at each depth. Digits and noise near the input; an epic register through the middle; a storyteller's voice by the end.

Read the middle layers loosely. The published lens is a fit checkpoint averaged over 80 prompts of generic web text — short of the convergence threshold its own config set. Broad trends across the stack are solid; fine distinctions between adjacent layers are not.
