Qwen3-32B · Jacobian lens · refusal

The Refusal Direction

A single difference-of-means direction, in the style of Arditi et al., transported through the Jacobian lens at every layer and decoded into the tokens it is disposed to make the model say. Read downward and it moves through three registers: the subject matter of harm, then its legal status, then the apology itself — arriving at `sorry` with the readout more confident than anywhere else in the stack.

The source needed some forensics. Its README states dimension 6656 — Qwen3-32B's residual stream is 5120 — and describes a single vector, but the file is a [5120] view into a [512, 5120] float64 storage that torch.save carried along: the whole 8-position × 64-layer candidate grid. Axis order was recovered from the norm profile (rising with depth, Spearman 0.99; transposed, 0.08). Provenance is third-party and unverified — the readout below is the evidence, since a mislabelled direction would not verbalize refusal. The lens itself is an 80-prompt fit, so read broad trends rather than adjacent layers.
