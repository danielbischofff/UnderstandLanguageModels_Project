Overall effect across eight personas

Each cell is the mean paired steered − unsteered difference: average all twelve questions within each persona, then give all eight personas equal weight. Each row is a separate configuration (96 persona–question pairs).

The four metrics remain separate. Specificity is the intended persona score minus the mean of seven rival persona scores in the same bank, compared between steering and control. Persona-specific word banks differ; this is a descriptive average for the selected personas, not a shared literal word count.

Cells show mean difference, exploratory 95% interval, and number of personas with a positive mean (out of eight). Green shading marks a positive overall mean; it does not imply statistical significance.

Intervals use 10,000 resamples of twelve question IDs with replacement, keeping all eight persona pairs together for each sampled question. Personas are fixed and are not resampled. With only twelve deliberately selected questions, these intervals describe sensitivity to question composition and do not establish population-wide generalization. No correction for multiple comparisons is applied. β = 0 pins the persona projection; β = 0.25 allows a band around the target.
