Each point or bar compares one steered answer with its unsteered answer on the same question. Scores are averaged over retained target words and cached answer positions before subtraction.

Positive differences indicate higher association under steering. Green bars mark positive differences, red bars negative differences; the dashed horizontal line is the equal-question mean. In the all-persona view, colors identify personas and the zero line separates increases from decreases. Missing differences are omitted, never replaced with zero. All twelve questions are included.
