\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
In our analysis, we found many examples for a steered personality's point of view showing up in the J-Lens on user prompt tokens. These examples excludes baseline tokens discussed in the previous section, that widely showed up accross all tokens and questions regardless of context. While each individual example is anecdotal, we believe they provide together substantial evidence that supports our hypothesis. 
\subsection{Tylenol Question}
Before we look at the J-Lens, we verified that the steering changed the model's style and behavior as expected by inspecting the model's responses. In the Appendix, \ref{sec:TylenolAnswers} we show the analysis of the assistant's responses, both in alpha steering and in band capping methods. We discuss the J-Lens observations below. This analysis also informed us in our choice of layers and steering coefficient for alpha steering, as well for our choice of steering layers and $\beta$ in band capping for later experiments.

The full analysis of the words that were either suppressed or showed up in the J-Lens in the user prompt, for the Tylenol scenario and for other scenarios can be found in \href{https://claude.ai/artifact/TBJkL58Goy6S8YqizsirQF}{this claude artifact}.
