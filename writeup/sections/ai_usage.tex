\section*{AI Usage Declaration}
\label{sec:ai-usage}

Throughout our project, we heavily relied on AI tools (such as Codex and Claude Code) for writing code, and for ideating on the steering methods, and on the analysis of our results. \textbf{The write-up is exclusively human}, and peer reviewed, with the following exceptions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Limited use of GitHub Copilot's auto-complete in some places.
    \item The creation of the band-cap figure and some of its caption text.
    \item Spotting syntactical or grammar mistakes and fixing them.
    \item Some of the text in Appendix \ref{sec:questions} detailing the different questions.
\end{itemize}
